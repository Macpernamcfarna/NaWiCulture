"Our Mission & Vision"

Preserving the rich tapestry of Sierra Leone's heritage for future generations

Our Mission

To document, preserve, and promote Sierra Leone's diverse cultural heritage—from the Krio legacy of Freetown to the traditions of the Mende, Temne, Limba, and all our ethnic groups—through innovative digital platforms that educate and inspire both local and global audiences.

Our Vision

To become the premier digital repository of Sierra Leonean culture, connecting Salone people worldwide to their roots while introducing our rich heritage—from the cotton tree to the provinces—to the global community.
